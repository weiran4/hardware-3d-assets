# SEL-2488 exterior reference in the GTSYNCv2 bench

On [course page 33](https://weiran4.github.io/hardware-3d-assets/#rtds-presentation-33),
select the PTP v2 Grandmaster source to see the two SEL-2488 inspection views.
The model is a separate, static specimen and never supplies a wiring endpoint.
Returning to the overview keeps the selected route and simulated clock state.

The [SEL product page](https://selinc.com/products/2488/) shows the photographed
[front panel](https://selinc.com/v3/assets/bltea08f3d94a418a1b/bltd411d8ecc3f3e830/6a27315bbf682b7ab98dd617/2488_Front.png?content_type=image%2Fpng)
and [rear panel](https://selinc.com/v3/assets/bltea08f3d94a418a1b/blt4428eeaab4b043f3/6a27315d07c3e9f8539d697f/2488_Back.png?content_type=image%2Fpng).
[Figure 8 in the datasheet](https://selinc.com/api/download/105748/#page=6)
gives the rack-mount dimensions: 482.6 × 43.7 × 248.6 mm (W × H × D).
The original mesh models the visible rack ears, LCD, indicators and Ethernet
management jack as well as the pictured rear with eight BNC outputs, six SMA
ports, four Ethernet jacks, separate antenna and IRIG-B inputs, COM and two
SEL-9330-C power supply modules. Labels and LCD markings are drawn locally;
the original photographs are linked, not bundled.

Options can change the rear panel. The static display and indicators are for
visual comparison, not measured values, and this is not a mounting drawing,
pinout, connection recommendation, or proof of compatibility with GTSYNCv2.
